- Domain and zone bypass: names in .ru, .su and Cyrillic zones leave outside the tunnel, with no reconnect.
- Your own domains can be listed by hand; a rule covers subdomains too.
- Your own list of DNS resolvers: the order they are asked in, each one switchable, and the path they take.
- A separate DNS for one chosen app.
- Direct Flow: Russian apps always travel outside the tunnel, and the list is editable.
- Your own Cloudflare profiles are issued automatically half a minute after connecting, ahead of the bundled ones.
- Proton reworked: nodes are picked by country and latency, and no profiles no longer means no tunnel.
- Opera, Proton and Cloudflare registration goes through our own relay, so it works where their APIs are blocked.
- Main screen: protocol and region as buttons, a latency graph, and an honest line under the status.
- A notification screen: the speed and transport shown in the shade can be turned off.
- A home-screen widget: connect, and jump to the next profile.
- The TOR button collects working bridges (obfs4, webtunnel, vanilla, snowflake). The Tor transport itself is not here yet.
- The app is almost 10 MB smaller.
