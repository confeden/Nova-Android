- Tor is now a transport: the TOR button brings up a tunnel and app traffic goes through the Tor network.
- The entry is selectable: WEBTUNNEL, OBFS4, VANILLA or no bridges, ordered by how likely each is to work from Russia.
- Bridges are collected and liveness-checked by the app itself.
- A hint about the system Private DNS: in strict mode no name resolves over Tor, and the app explains what to switch.
- Choosing a protocol, a region or a Tor entry while the VPN is off no longer starts a connection on its own.
- DNS screen: an explicit reorder mode, and a transport choice per resolver - Any, DoH or DoT, Any by default.
- The notification is expanded with a Disconnect button; a new setting collapses it back.
- Ten themes with a picker; settings split into six groups with icons; the selected protocol button glows in the theme colour.
- Configurations: a Built-in / Imported choice instead of a toggle button.
- A 2x1 home-screen widget whose round buttons no longer get clipped.
- Manual tunnel MTU, 1000-1440 in steps of 10, 1280 by default.
- Logs are anonymised: the network stays visible, the host does not; each session starts with build, Android and device.
- Dialog buttons are no longer clipped along their top edge.
- The Personal profiles card and the Moscow Cloudflare node switch are gone from Settings.
